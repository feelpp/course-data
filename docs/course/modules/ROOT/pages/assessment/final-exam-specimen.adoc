This public specimen fixes the structure, depth, and point balance. The live examination changes the context, values, and selected P1 method while preserving equivalence.

== Conditions

* 2 hours 45 minutes, individual, 40 points, AI Mode A.
* CPU-only, self-contained and offline-capable environment.
* Submit written responses, code, tests, and the generated `result.json`.
* P2/P3 content is not required.

== Scenario

An environmental laboratory provides `samples.csv`, `sites.csv`, and a source card. Each sample has a site, collection time, instrument batch, numeric measurements, and a binary review outcome. The data contain one repeated sample key, a unit inconsistency, structured missingness, and a post-review field. The laboratory wants a reproducible screening analysis, not an autonomous operational decision.

== A. Data audit and pipeline repair — 10 points, P0

. State the unit, target, intended use, and one risk of the proposed use. (2)
. Validate the site join and repair the repeated key/unit issue with executable evidence. (3)
. Identify the post-review leakage and explain why deleting only its obvious name may be insufficient. (2)
. Repair a provided workflow that globally imputes/scales before splitting. (3)

== B. Mathematical and method reasoning — 10 points, P1

. Derive or explain the link between one stated probabilistic assumption and its loss. (2)
. A design matrix has nearly dependent columns. Explain why explicit inversion is unsafe and select a stable solver or regularised alternative. (2)
. Interpret a Ridge/Lasso or bias–variance result and propose one controlled diagnostic. (2)
. Answer one announced method question: tree regularisation, PCA/SVD interpretation, or clustering objective and limits. (4)

The live paper selects one of the announced method families; it does not require every family in one sitting.

== C. Implementation and tests — 10 points, P0/P1

. Implement one transformation with a clear input/output contract. (3)
. Place learned preprocessing and the estimator in a validation-safe pipeline. (3)
. Write one toy-oracle unit test and one identity/schema test. (2)
. Produce `result.json` with data ID, seed, split ID, model ID, primary metric, and assessed commit. (2)

== D. Evaluation and interpretation — 6 points, P0/P1

. Select a metric and, when relevant, threshold from the stated error consequences. (2)
. Interpret a confusion matrix/residual or cluster-stability result. (2)
. State one uncertainty or variation measure and two limits on generalisation. (2)

== E. Reproducibility and governance — 4 points, P0

. Check the provided source/licence card and state one privacy or purpose constraint. (1)
. Explain how the lock, data checksum, seed, and assessed commit support rerunning the result. (2)
. Complete the Mode A assistance declaration. (1)

== Scoring safeguards

Each section receives independent partial credit. A test-leaking workflow caps the pipeline item at one point until corrected. An invalid row-multiplying join caps its join item at one point. A high metric cannot compensate for these failures. Counts are exact; deterministic floats use `rtol=1e-7`, `atol=1e-10`; seeded model metrics use absolute tolerance 0.01.

The complete reference run must remain under 15 minutes and the prepared student compute path under 8 minutes on the xref:technical-contract.adoc[reference environment]. Fixed data and iteration budgets are used; hardware speed is not graded.
