The same reasoning standards apply across controls, project work, and the final examination. Individual papers state the exact point allocation.

[cols="3,1,4,4",options="header"]
|===
|Criterion |Typical share |Competent evidence |Critical or insufficient evidence
|R1 — Problem and data understanding |10% |Correct unit, target, stakeholders, scope, assumptions, and success criterion. |Wrong unit of analysis or an answer to a different problem.
|R2 — Data quality and provenance |15% |Traceable permitted data; checked schema, joins, types, units, missingness, duplicates, anomalies. |Untraceable data, invalid join, or silent unit/type error.
|R3 — Pipeline and leakage control |20% |Splits protect evaluation; learned transformations and selection are fitted inside the training design. |Test-informed preprocessing or selection, target/temporal/group leakage.
|R4 — Method and mathematical reasoning |15% |Baseline, assumptions, stable computation, and method choice are connected. |No baseline, incompatible assumptions, or numerically unsafe implementation without discussion.
|R5 — Evaluation and uncertainty |15% |Consequences guide metrics and thresholds; failures, variation, and uncertainty are reported. |Accuracy alone under imbalance, repeated test tuning, no failure analysis.
|R6 — Code, tests, and reproducibility |15% |Toy-oracle tests, smoke path, clean run, fixed environment, seeds and data identifiers. |Hidden notebook state, no executable checks, cannot run from the assessed commit.
|R7 — Interpretation, limitations, communication, AI disclosure |10% |Claims match evidence; figures are labelled; non-causality and limits are explicit; assistance is disclosed. |Causal claims from association, material omissions, prohibited or undisclosed assistance.
|===

== Performance levels

* *Excellent:* correct, reproducible, well-tested evidence; choices follow from assumptions and consequences; uncertainty and limitations are explicit.
* *Competent:* correct core pipeline and interpretation with minor omissions that do not invalidate the conclusion.
* *Developing:* partial technical success, but validation, testing, or justification is incomplete and conclusions need qualification.
* *Insufficient:* a critical Essential (P0) failure, non-reproducible evidence, major leakage/join error, untraceable data, or unsupported conclusion.

== Fairness and partial credit

Points attach to observable evidence, not to a single final number. A failed downstream model does not erase valid data-audit or test work. Conversely, a high score produced by invalid data or evaluation cannot recover the capped validity criterion. Rubrics use fixed computation budgets and the tolerances in xref:technical-contract.adoc[the execution contract].
