The static course site does not require student accounts and does not intentionally collect personal data. Hosting and linked third-party services may process standard connection information according to their own policies.

Do not place personal, confidential, sensitive, or research-restricted data in public issues, pull requests, notebooks, or datasets. Use approved university services and the course instructions for protected submissions.
