This course prepares you to turn heterogeneous data into reproducible and defensible evidence for scientific and industrial decisions.

== What you are expected to do

* identify the unit of analysis, target, stakeholders, assumptions, and risks;
* audit provenance, licence, privacy, schema, units, missingness, duplicates, anomalies, and representativeness;
* implement validated transformations, joins, reshaping, grouping, and feature pipelines;
* choose leakage-safe splits, baselines, metrics, thresholds, and uncertainty summaries;
* test transformations and rerun the analysis from a clean checkout;
* communicate limitations and declare AI assistance.
