# Source archive policy

`archive/raw/` is a local, non-published preservation area. It is excluded from Git because the imported material has mixed or unknown provenance and may contain instructor solutions or embargoed assessments.

The source inventory records checksums, original paths, provenance status, and proposed dispositions. No item from the raw archive may be copied into the public course tree until its source, owner, licence or permission, intended use, and student/instructor status have been resolved.
